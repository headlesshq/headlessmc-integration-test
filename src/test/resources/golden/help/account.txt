Usage: headlessmc account [-hV] [-p=<provider>] [COMMAND]
Manage your account.
  -h, --help      Show this help message and exit.
  -p, --provider=<provider>
                  The authentication provider to use (default, offline,
                    yggdrasil, ...)
  -V, --version   Print version information and exit.
Commands:
  login       Log into an account.
  list, ls    List your accounts.
  remove, rm  Removes an account.
  select      Select the primary account to use.
  refresh     Refreshes an accounts token.
